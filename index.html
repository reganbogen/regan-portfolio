Selected systems

IMXP HQ

IMXP · Company operations platform

The operating system for the company, built from scratch. It syncs every sale, application and guest from the event platform every 15 minutes and is the source of truth for physical inventory, enforcing shared limits on rooms, cabins and passes so overlapping offers can never oversell. Around that core:

  • Rooming: places guests into Nile cruise cabins and hotel rooms on real deck and site plans, reads onboarding answers to suggest roommates and flag conflicts, and tracks travel requests.
  • Deals: a sales CRM that follows each buyer live from first contact to payment, with stages, owners, partners, targets, a pipeline report, and Slack and email updates.
  • Marketing: one dashboard for ad spend, cost per sign-up by channel, email audiences, a map of site traffic, Google search data, and a radar that checks how ChatGPT and Gemini answer questions about the event.
  • Bespoke offers: custom group packages built through Claude, each with its own proposal page and checkout.

Deterministic, tested and audited where money and inventory are involved; AI only drafts, and a person decides. Phone-ready, with per-person access for staff and outside partners.

AI support desk

IMXP · Customer email agent

An agent that works the event support inbox. It answers the questions the website and FAQ already cover, from a knowledge base rebuilt nightly from the live checkout, and fact-checks every reply against it before sending. It resends receipts on request, holds any answer that cites a date about to pass, and hands everything else to the right person with a summary and a draft. Staff stay in control from Gmail: reply and it steps back, label a thread to give it to the bot or keep it away.
